Style directions: a proposal

Three colour directions in the same parts, light and dark side by side. Sizes S / M / L are 28 / 36 / 44px; every bordered control of a size shares its height. Contrast is measured live: body text AAA (7:1), other text AA (4.5:1), borders, focus and icons 3:1. Nothing here is wired to the app.
