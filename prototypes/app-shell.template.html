Design proposal · app structure

Campaign App Shell

The proposed structure: Sheet (your existing character sheet, kept as it is), a new Combat view for what you need mid-fight, and a DM tab only the DM sees. Sheet and DM are the real tools, loaded with Jaque. Combat reads Jaque's ability scores, HP, AC and class from the Sheet tab, so change his Dex there and come back. Nothing here declares or locks in actions; you still say what you do out loud.
