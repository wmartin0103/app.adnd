**Proposed Revisions to BLADE (revised Bard kit, AD&D 2E)**

With the intent being you don’t have to Dual Class anymore.

**Description**

Blades are master artisans of the knife and the sword. Their art is the weapon itself: a Blade's performance is a display of skill and control in which knives and blades flash about his body with deadly precision, tossed, caught, spun, and thrown, never quite touching skin. Blades also perform slow, elegant weapon dances of thrusts, lunges, leaps, and graceful arcs. Crowds gather for both, and both are exactly as dangerous as they look.

Blades carry a reputation as the deadliest weapon masters in the land. For most of their history that reputation ran well ahead of the truth: the dance is not the duel, and a juggler of knives is not a killer. This Blade is different. He has studied where the armor gaps and where the tendons run, and he fights the way he performs, with speed and a surgeon's eye rather than brute strength. The reputation, in his case, is earned.

Blades are travelers, showmen, and hired steel. Common folk enjoy the act and then keep an eye on their purses and their backs, and more than one Blade has been taken for an assassin.

**Requirements:** Dex 13, Int 14, Cha 13. Any non-lawful alignment.

**Secondary Skills:** Armorer, Bowyer/Fletcher, Gambler, or Weaponsmith.

**Weapon Proficiencies:** Proficiency ONLY in: sabre, cutlass, rapier, short blades, whip, hand crossbow, light crossbow. Anything else at the normal non-proficiency penalty. At least half of his weapon proficiencies must go to short blades, and he may learn only one weapon that can't be used in melee (i.e. one of the two crossbows). No bonus weapon proficiency; his signature weapon must be one of his starting proficiencies.

**Nonweapon Proficiencies:** Bonus: Blind-fighting, Juggling, Local History, Reading/Writing (native tongue). Recommended: Dancing, Disguise, Etiquette, Gaming, Intimidation, Rope Use, Tumbling.

**Armor/Equipment:** Leather or studded leather only, no shields. Must spend at least half of starting money on signature weapon and armor.

**SPECIAL BENEFITS**

*Signature Weapon.* At 1st level the Blade picks one weapon from his list and specializes as a fighter: +1 to hit, +2 damage, 3/2 attacks (2/1 at 7th, 5/2 at 13th). He may never specialize in a second weapon.

*Duelist's Precision.* With any weapon on his list:

• In melee, he uses his Dexterity missile attack adjustment in place of the Strength to-hit adjustment.

• Against a FLANKED or UNAWARE opponent, he uses Intelligence in place of Strength for the damage adjustment, in melee or with a crossbow. Flanked = already in melee with two enemies (a competent fighter can always keep two opponents in front of him; three is where he loses that). Unaware = surprised, attacked from hiding, or otherwise not expecting him. Against an opponent engaged by fewer than that, he uses Strength as normal. Int never grants an exceptional-strength bonus; caps at +2 for Int 18.

If Strength would give a better result in either case, use Strength.

*Handle Weapons.* Blades train and perform with a weapon in each hand and are considered ambidextrous. With two weapons from his list: no penalty on the primary weapon, -2 on the off-hand, regardless of Dex. Only the primary may be the signature weapon. When catching a small thrown weapon with the Juggling proficiency, he adds his level to the roll.

*Weapon Display.* By whirling a weapon about his body in a display of skill, precision, and deadly grace, the Blade affects the morale and courage of those who watch. One full round, no attack. Choose one effect:

• Intimidation: functions as a use of the Intimidation nonweapon proficiency, same effects and target rules. A Blade without the proficiency may still attempt it through the display as an unmodified Charisma check. A Blade who HAS Intimidation gets +2 to the check when made through a weapon display.

• Fascination: onlookers not already in combat who fail a save vs. paralyzation watch the display (up to 1 round/level) and take no other action. Any attack or obvious threat breaks it.

*Influence Reactions.* As the bard ability, but performed as a weapon display rather than a song.

*Inspire Allies.* As the bard ability, performed as a full round of weapon display before combat. That display can't also be used for Intimidation or Fascination.

*Offensive Spin.* One full round of menacing weapon display directed at a single opponent, making no attack. Next round he makes a normal attack against that opponent; on a hit, the victim must immediately make a morale check, with the usual consequences for failure. Wasted if the Blade is forced to move, attack, or defend himself during the display round, or if the attack misses.

*Defensive Spin.* A continuous defensive pattern. He may not attack, but AC improves by half his level (round up), and any creature that attacks him must save vs. paralyzation or take damage equal to half his level (round up, max = his weapon's maximum damage). Lasts up to a number of rounds equal to his Constitution; ends immediately if he's hit.

*Trick Throw.* With a thrown knife or dagger, or a hand or light crossbow, he may make a called shot at only -2 (instead of -4) to pin a sleeve or cloak, cut a rope or strap, knock a small object from a hand, or land a blade within an inch of a target without harm. DM adjudicates on success; on a failure the shot misses. Trick shots only, not called shots meant to wound.

*Limited Legend Lore.* Legend Lore for WEAPONS only: the lore of a named blade, a famous smith's work, a weapon's enchantment by reputation, at the normal chance for his level. Nothing else.

*Thief Skills.* Climb Walls 50%, Detect Noise 20%, Hide in Shadows 5%, Move Silently 10%. 20 discretionary points at 1st level, 15 per level after, plus racial and Dex modifiers. No Pick Pockets, no Read Languages.

**SPECIAL HINDRANCES**

*Delayed Spellcasting.* First spells at 4th level. From then on he casts as a bard two levels lower than his actual level (spells per day and max spell level). Spellbook rules and Int limits unchanged.

*No Counter-Song.* He doesn't sing, and a spinning blade does nothing against a harpy's song.

*No Scroll Use.* Never gains the bard's 10th-level ability to read and use magical scrolls of any class.

*Restricted Arsenal.* Weapon list is fixed: Saber/Cutlass/Rapier, knives, short blades, the whip, and crossbows, whatever the campaign puts in front of him.

*Light Armor Only.* If he wears anything heavier than studded leather he loses Duelist's Precision, Handle Weapons, Weapon Display, both Spins, and all thief skills until it's removed.

*Reputation.* Guards, magistrates, and clergy in lawful communities react at -2 to a known Blade, and he's the first one questioned when a body turns up.

**Wealth Options:** Standard bard, subject to the equipment requirement above.

**Races:** Any race that may be a bard. Mostly humans and half-elves; halflings take readily to the knife-work.

**Design Notes:** Trades the scholar's half of the bard for a duelist's. Dex and Int both become offensive stats. With 18 in each and a specialized short sword he attacks at +3 for d6+4 against a flanked or unaware target — fighter-like numbers — but only under those conditions; in a fair fight he's at d6+2, capped at d4/d6 base damage, on the rogue THAC0 table, in light armor. The two-ally flanking rule means the bonus is a reward for setup or stealth, not an every-round thing. Spells arrive late and stay two levels behind; that's the main price. Handle Weapons and both Spins follow the original kit; Offensive Spin is a fear effect, not an attack buff, so specialization is the only source of extra attacks. If it plays too strong, pull the extra attack from specialization first, the Dex-to-hit second.

## Table clarifications (not in the original document)

- **Multi-weapon fighting stays.** A Blade fighting with two weapons gets **one
  extra off-hand attack per round**, on top of the Signature Weapon's attacks
  per round. Handle Weapons sets its penalty (−2).
- **The off hand can hold any weapon** (normal non-proficiency penalty if he
  isn't proficient in it), but the extra attack only comes **when the main hand
  holds his specialized weapon** (a dagger). That's what "only the primary may
  be the signature weapon" means at this table.
- **Signature Weapon covers the whole proficiency.** Every dagger gets +1 to
  hit and +2 damage, off-hand included. The off-hand attack is still just the
  one extra attack; it never gets the Signature Weapon's extra attacks per round.
- **No mixing melee and ranged within the Signature Weapon's attacks** in the
  same round: all of them are melee, or all are thrown. The extra off-hand
  attack is exempt and can be either.
- **Flanked = the table's Flanking chip.** Turning on Flanking (+2 to hit) also
  counts the target as flanked for Duelist's Precision damage.
- **Thief skills a Blade has:** Climb Walls, Detect Noise, Hide in Shadows,
  Move Silently. Race and Dex adjust only skills he has, so there's no Pick
  Pockets, Read Languages, or Find/Remove Traps from race or Dex alone.
- **Locksmithing grants full Open Locks** (points, race, Dex, and misc
  bonuses all apply). Locksmithing and thieves' tools each add +10%.
  The character sheet and the converter both follow this.
- **Signature Weapon thrown rate (from Jaque's sheet):** 3/1, 4/1 at 7th,
  5/1 at 13th.

## How the app models it

| Rule | In the app |
|---|---|
| Signature Weapon +1/+2 | Combat bonus on the weapon category (every dagger, either hand) |
| Signature attacks per round | On the Signature Weapon bonus, so every main-hand dagger gets it, by level: melee 3/2 → 2/1 (7th) → 5/2 (13th); thrown 3/1 → 4/1 → 5/1 |
| Duelist's Precision (to hit) | Melee uses Dex missile adj. instead of Str, unless Str is better |
| Duelist's Precision (damage) | With the "Flanking" or "Target unaware" chip on: Int replaces Str for damage in melee or with a crossbow, unless Str is better; capped at +2 |
| Handle Weapons | Off-hand attacks at −2 |
| Multi-weapon fighting | One extra off-hand attack per round with any weapon (not bows), only with a dagger in the main hand (`__combatRules.offHandAttack.needsMain`) |
| Weapon proficiencies | `__weaponProficiencies` (Jaque: daggers); anything else at the rogue non-proficiency penalty (−3) |
| Defensive Spin | Toggle: no attack, AC better by half his level (rounded up) |
| Offensive Spin, Weapon Display | Toggles: no attack this round |
| Trick Throw | A custom thrown attack with −2 |
| Thief skills | Only the four Blade skills, plus Open Locks with Locksmithing |
| Delayed spellcasting | Spells cast as a bard two levels lower (caster level). Turned on by the class settings → Custom Kit Rules → **Blade**; older saves go by a class name containing "Blade" |
